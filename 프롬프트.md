Build a small browser painting app in the current folder and use it to paint the portrait in reference.png, as close to the photo as you can.



THE APP

\- A clean, minimal paint program: top bar with the title "Paint" and a live stroke counter; a toolbar with brush size, opacity and the current color swatch; a large canvas on the left with exactly the same proportions as reference.png, starting completely blank; on the right a small "Reference" panel showing reference.png and a short label with the current stage (Blocking in / Form and color / Refinement / Fine details).

\- Plain HTML, CSS and JS with a canvas. No frameworks, no external assets, no image-generation models or services.



THE PAINTING

\- The final image must be made only of individual brush strokes drawn on the canvas with a soft round or bristle brush. Never draw the photo itself onto the visible canvas with drawImage, putImageData, a CSS image, a filter or a mask.

\- You may analyze reference.png with code to plan the painting.

\- Paint like a real artist, in layers: huge rough shapes first, then medium strokes that build form and color, then smaller strokes, then tiny details on the eyes, mouth, hands and hair edges. Strokes should follow the direction of the forms instead of sitting on a regular grid.

\- Pay special attention to what makes him recognisable: the sharp jawline and cheekbones, the deep-set eyes and heavy brows, the brooding expression, the messy swept-up hair with its loose strands, light stubble. Then the black tuxedo: crisp white shirt collar, bow tie, satin lapels catching the light. Keep the skin tones warm and the blacks deep but not flat.



DONE MEANS

\- One command starts it on a local static server (tell me which); no console errors.

\- Compare the finished canvas with reference.png yourself and keep refining the stroke plan until you cannot improve the likeness further.

\- Do not deploy or push anywhere.
